How it works

AI decides what to use. The physics runs on real formulas.

  1. 01

    Pick a topic or scan a textbook page

    For example Physics, Ohm's law. Or take a photo of the page and the AI finds the topic.

  2. 02

    AI builds the 3D experiment

    It picks the right tools from the library and sets up the lab and a short task, in seconds.

  3. 03

    The AI tutor guides the student in Uzbek

    The student builds, connects and measures in 3D. The tutor asks guiding questions instead of giving answers.

  4. 04

    The teacher gets an AI report on every student

    Every step, mistake and minute is analyzed, plus a summary for the whole class.

A personal report for every student

Spend less time grading and on paperwork. The AI drafts reports and feedback; you just review and approve.
